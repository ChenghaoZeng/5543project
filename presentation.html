What is Housing Stress?

  • Defined as spending >30% of household weekly income on weekly housing costs
  • reflects financial vulnerability

Key Predictor Interpretations

  1. Household Size:
    Larger households reduce housing stress (1-person increase → ~ 1.8% lower odds).

  2. Age of Youngest Child:
    Older children may slightly lower stress (1 year older → ~1.2% lower odds).

  3. Purchase Price of Dwelling: Higher dwelling purchase price increase stress ($100,000 increase → ~1.3% higher odds).

  4. Tenure Type: Compared to owners with no mortgage —

  • Owners with mortgage: ~6% higher odds of stress.
  • Pivate renters: ~ 2.1% higher odds of stress.

Conclusion and Recommendations:

Conclusion:

Lone person under 35, families with dependents, and females remain most vulnerable due to rising housing costs and limited financial buffers.

Recommendations:

  1. Expand Social & Affordable Housing
  • provide more social housing that are affordable to young adults.
  1. Targeted Financial Assistance
  • focus on groups most exposed to housing cost burdens.
